# Initial Findings

- Dataset: 56,000 customers, 28 original columns.
- Data check: no blank cells found; customer IDs are unique.
- Enrollment dates: 2012-07-30 to 2014-06-29.
- Age uses 2014 as the reference year.
- Overall campaign response rate: 14.76% (8,265 customers).
- High-spender threshold: total spend above 1,564 (90th percentile).
- High spenders: 5,599 customers; response rate 26.04%.
- Other customers: response rate 13.51%.
- High visits and below-average spend segment: 20,686 customers; response rate 8.28%.
- Responders had higher average income and spend than non-responders.
- Responders also had higher average web, store, and catalog purchases.

## Notes to verify

- Dataset currency is not confirmed; do not label income or spend as rupees.
- Age is calculated as 2014 minus birth year.
- These are observed relationships, not proof of cause and effect.

## Segment results

- High spenders: 5,599 customers; response rate 26.04%, versus 13.51% for others.
- Age 60+ high spenders: 926 customers; response rate 27.21%.
- High visits (>5) and below-average spend (<640.33): 20,686 customers; response rate 8.28%, average visits 7.23.
- Country response rate: Australia 20.19% (5,677 customers); Canada 11.57% (10,746 customers).
- Education response rate: Graduation 17.30%; Basic 9.28%.
- Responders had higher average spend across all six product categories.
- Responders averaged more web, store, and catalog purchases; deal purchases were similar.
- Marital-status labels include Alone, YOLO, and Absurd. Confirm their meaning before drawing conclusions.

## Interpretation cautions

- Some high-rate segments are relatively small; consider both response rate and customer count.
- The high-visit, low-spend group is a possible opportunity for testing, not a proven target.
