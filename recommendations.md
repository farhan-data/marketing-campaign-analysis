# Marketing Campaign Recommendations

1. **High spenders ko priority dekar campaign test karein.**
   In 5,599 customers ka response rate 26.04% tha, baaki customers ke 13.51% ke muqable.

2. **High-spender age groups par targeted pilot chalayein.**
   60+ group mein 926 customers aur 27.21% response rate tha; 30–44 group mein 2,024 customers aur 26.24% response rate tha.

3. **High website visits, low spend wale customers ke liye test karein.**
   Is segment mein 20,686 customers the, par response rate 8.28% tha. Personalized product suggestions ya landing-page offers ka chhota test chalaya ja sakta hai.

4. **Web, store, aur catalog channels par tailored messaging test karein.**
   Responders ke average web, store, aur catalog purchases zyada the; deal purchases lagbhag barabar the. Isliye sabko blanket discount dene ke bajay channel-specific offers test karein.

5. **Australia aur India mein campaign pilot test karein.**
   Response rates 20.19% aur 18.76% the. Budget badhane se pehle conversion value aur campaign cost compare karein.
